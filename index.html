Our Programs

What We Do
Every Single Day

Education

We enroll children in accredited schools, provide uniforms, books, and after-school tutoring — ensuring no child falls behind due to poverty.

340+ children currently enrolled in school

Healthcare

Monthly medical check-ups, vaccinations, nutritional support, and mental health care — because a healthy child can truly learn and grow.

1,200+ medical visits covered last year

Community

We build family-like homes and connect children with caregivers, mentors, and community support systems that last a lifetime.
